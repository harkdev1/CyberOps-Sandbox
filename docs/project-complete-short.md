# CyberOps Sandbox

Status: Active lab / practice environment

## Project story
CyberOps Sandbox was built as a hands-on cybersecurity practice environment for defensive operations, detection work, triage, and operational documentation. The goal was to create a realistic lab environment where exercises and investigations could be repeated, reviewed, and referenced without losing the evidence trail.

## Key achievements
- Established a structured cybersecurity lab workflow for repeatable exercises and investigations.
- Organized the workspace around documentation, evidence capture, and technical review.
- Created a reliable project trail through logs, screenshots, and notes for future retrospective work.
- Kept the environment practical and reviewable for real-world operational learning.

## Lessons learned
- Documentation is part of the work, not a separate task.
- Evidence should be captured at the moment the work happens.
- A clean operational record makes follow-up work faster and easier to trust.
- Consistency matters more than speed when building a technical lab.

## Next steps
- Continue building operational scenarios around detection, response, and recovery.
- Expand the evidence trail with stronger lab runbooks and summaries.
- Keep the README and status file aligned with current focus and progress.
- Turn lessons learned into repeatable playbooks for future exercises.

## Proof points
- The project is organized around logs, screenshots, and a documented workflow.

- The repo is structured like a real operational workspace: reviewable, traceable, and easy to revisit.
